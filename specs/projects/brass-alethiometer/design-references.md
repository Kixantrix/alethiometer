# Design references: brass alethiometer

| Reference | What we borrow | What we reject |
|---|---|---|
| Myth Made alethiometer build ([video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rpL9xWBeznU), [files](https://mythmade.gumroad.com/l/trhgos)) | Octagonal folded-brass box (~108 mm), three side thumbwheels on scalloped wheels, an X27 needle, the hand-drawn symbol set | A random prop needle (ours reads the hands and answers); 3D-printed internals; closed thumbwheel slots that need tilting to fit |
| BBC *His Dark Materials* prop | Ivory ring, sepia silhouettes, gilt rules, blued hands, a crown at the corner | — |
| Watch and clock construction | Nested arbors (cannon, hour/minute pipes), cheese/countersunk case screws, crown with retaining clip | Watchmaker-grade hand fitting (see builder profile) |
| The website (`js/`, `api/`) | Symbol order, oracle, reading format, needle choreography (sweep, swings, pauses) | — |

Myth Made's art is not redistributable. The dial generator takes it from the owner's local
download and writes to a git-ignored folder (D-003).
