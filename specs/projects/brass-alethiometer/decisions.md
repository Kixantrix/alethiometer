# Decisions: brass alethiometer

> Settled questions, with the owner's own words. Don't reopen one without new information;
> if you supersede one, add a new entry that names the old one. Format: [dfma §7](../../../.github/skills/dfma/SKILL.md).
> Source: Copilot session "Brass alethiometer hardware" (2026-11-21/22).

| ID | Decision | Owner's words | Why / notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| D-001 | Thumbwheels gear-drive the three framing hands mechanically; each hand is read by an AS5600 on a 1:1 sensor gear. Only the answer needle is motorised (X27.168, 2:1 gear pair, homes on its stop). | "You do what you think is right that makes the object as good as possible." | Authentic feel, no homing for the hands, works if the knobs move while asleep. |
| D-002 | Case is SendCutSend laser-cut and bent .040" brass: an octagon based on Myth Made's folded box, split into a lower tray and a telescoping upper shell, with a hinged lid. | Initial brief: "main construction ... brass that can be ordered from a cnc like send cut send". | The split lets the thumbwheels drop straight in and the skirt hides height tolerance. |
| D-003 | Dial uses Myth Made's hand-drawn symbols. Garland is renamed Salamander everywhere (site, API, firmware, dial). | "I definitely prefer their symbols." / chose rename everywhere. | The art isn't redistributable, so it is generated into git-ignored `hardware/dial/local/`. Never commit it (public repo). |
| D-004 | Electronics live on one through-hole carrier PCB with test pads and holes for every off-board wire. | "I'd prefer printed circuit board to hand wiring for ease of assembly ... All throughholes ... easy for me to debug". | KiCad generated from the shared `layout.py`. |
| D-005 | Every module plugs into 5 mm sockets; the X27 is soldered after its mesh is set. | "A sounds reasonable ... I want to design ease of use/assembly/iteration into this design if things need to change." | Adds about 5 mm of height; serviceability wins. |
| D-006 | Fits are sized for SendCutSend's worst case (±0.13 mm cut, ±0.25–0.5 mm bends) so nothing needs filing. | "...wouldn't mind having a bit more room for it and have things still work without me needing to dremel everywhere" | One tolerance-budget block in `design.py`. |
| D-007 | Shafts run in tube bushings, with tight gear bores. Gears stay with SendCutSend. | "Your call on the exact method. As long as it's still easy for me to assemble and doesn't cost a huge amount more." | Bushing install is being superseded by turned parts (D-013). |
| D-008 | Crown switch and wall USB-C are mounted on the carrier PCB; the crown is centred on the tray's corner chamfer. | "that makes sense. Then you could also have it more centered on the corner" | Nothing is glued to the wall. |
| D-009 | Lid opens flat to 180°; the hinge's own leaves are the stop. No extra stop hardware. | Chose this option over keeping the 135° stop. | The 135° stop needed blocks, brackets and a rod. Example of relaxing a costly requirement. |
| D-010 | Buy the Guden stock brass hinge and cut it yourself with a Dremel, using the laser-cut trim gauge and drill guide. | "I suspect asking someone else to cut the hinge to size will be harder and more expensive" / "6. Seems worth it for now unless you find a hinge that would just work." | Supplier fit is on hold until a sample is in hand. A ready-to-fit hinge would supersede this. |
| D-011 | Gear train is module 0.8 (a stock size). Buy the three thumbwheels (Misumi brass, 26T, 5 mm face, 4 mm bore); laser-cut the thin gears. | "Why aren't we able to use stock sizes?" then "A but if you can buy that's better" | Module 0.7 was never purchasable. Confirm the Misumi configuration before ordering. |
| D-012 | Framing hands are SendCutSend blue-tempered spring steel (.015" only). | "1 is fine." | Removes the chemical darkening step. The hand stack is recomputed for the thinner stock. |
| D-013 | A turned-parts kit (CNC brass arbors, shafts, bushings, cannon) with shoulders, so every gear and hand is pushed on until it stops. One-piece turned crown held by an E-clip. | "4. I can cut but I CANNOT do it precisely. I am not a watchmaker." / "Yes: turned parts kit + one-piece crown + cheese-head case screws + spring-steel hands" | In progress on the hardware branch. A real quote is still needed (estimate $100–250). |
| D-014 | **Supersedes the cheese-head part of D-013.** External case screws stay countersunk. ASSEMBLY gives the starting hole size, the exact tool and the finished rim size. | "for external screws I still want those countersinked. I think I can do it easily ... make sure that the holes are the right size to start and you say the right tool" | SendCutSend can't countersink .040" brass, so the owner does it by hand (ledger: accepted). |
| D-015 | BOM keeps a tools list with the step each tool is for. | "keep a list in the BoM of tools needed as well" | Source of truth: `assembly-ledger.yaml` `tools` (`--tools-table`). |
| D-016 | The viewer shows the real KiCad board (holes, silkscreen, components) and every modelled fastener. | "it would also be good to get the real PCB geometry/look loaded in" | dfma §5 completeness. |
